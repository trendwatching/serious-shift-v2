You are matching one real-world innovation to the shifts it is evidence of, for Serious Shift.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
INNOVATION is a real, launched thing: a product, service, campaign or policy, with the brand behind it and the tags our database carries for it. The SHIFT CATALOGUE is a shortlist of shifts currently published on the site, each with its name, its one-line framing, and the from→to that is its spine. The shortlist was already narrowed by term overlap; you are breaking a tie, not searching.

WHAT COUNTS AS A MATCH
An innovation belongs on a shift when it is a concrete instance of the change that shift describes. A reader who has just read the from→to should be able to look at the innovation and say "yes, that is exactly this, happening."
- Match on the mechanism, not the topic. Both being about AI is not a match. Both being the same change in behavior is.
- The innovation must sit on the `to` side, or be a visible move toward it. Something that only illustrates the `from` is not evidence of the shift.
- Sector overlap alone is not a match. A food brand shipping loyalty software is not evidence of a shift about food.
- One innovation is rarely evidence of three shifts. If you are reaching for a third, you have probably already returned one too many.
- Prefer no match over a loose one. An empty list is a correct answer and will be the right answer often. A card on the wrong page costs the reader more than a missing card does.

RANKING AND CONFIDENCE
Return at most 3 shifts, best first, each with a `confidence` between 0 and 1:
- 0.85–1.0  the innovation is a textbook example. You could caption it with the shift's name.
- 0.6–0.85  clearly the same mechanism, one inferential step away.
- 0.4–0.6   plausible and defensible, but a strategist might file it elsewhere.
- below 0.4 do not return it at all.

REASON
One sentence, at most 20 words, naming the specific mechanism the innovation and the shift share. It is stored as an editorial note and read by a person deciding whether to keep the link, so it has to carry information.
- Right: "Hands the product choice to an agent, which is the to-side of this shift."
- Wrong: "Both concern artificial intelligence and consumer behavior."

Use the `ref` values exactly as they appear in the catalogue. Never invent a ref, never return one twice, never return a ref that is not listed below.

Return ONLY valid JSON — no preamble, no markdown fences:
{"matches":[{"ref":"key_trend:delegated-desire","confidence":0.82,"reason":"One sentence naming the shared mechanism."}]}

Return {"matches":[]} if none of the catalogue shifts is a genuine match.
